# Sources and indicator definitions

## Primary data provider
World Bank, World Development Indicators API: https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api
The app queries the official API endpoint pattern:
`https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/{ISO3}/indicator/{INDICATOR_CODE}?date={START}:{END}&format=json`

The retrieval date is generated by the app at runtime. The app requests the previous 70 complete calendar years, from current year minus 70 through current year minus 1. Coverage can be shorter because some indicators are not reported every year or for every country.

## Indicators
| Indicator | Code | Units | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Life expectancy at birth | `SP.DYN.LE00.IN` | years | Average years a newborn would live if current mortality patterns persisted. |
| Unemployment, national estimate | `SL.UEM.TOTL.NE.ZS` | % of labor force | National-estimate unemployment rate. |
| GDP per capita (current US$) | `NY.GDP.PCAP.CD` | current US dollars per person | GDP divided by midyear population. It is not average wealth or household income. |
| Crude birth rate | `SP.DYN.CBRT.IN` | births per 1,000 people per year | Annual rate, not a birth count. |
| Net migration | `SM.POP.NETM` | people over the period | Immigrants minus emigrants over the period, not an emigration count. |
| Intentional homicide rate | `VC.IHR.PSRC.P5` | per 100,000 people | Intentional homicides per 100,000 people. |
| Consumer price inflation | `FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG` | % annual change | Annual percentage change in the consumer price index. |
| Central government debt | `GC.DOD.TOTL.GD.ZS` | % of GDP | Central government debt as a share of GDP. |
| Total reserves including gold | `FI.RES.TOTL.CD` | current US dollars | Total reserves including gold. |
| External balance on goods and services | `NE.RSB.GNFS.ZS` | % of GDP | External balance on goods and services as a share of GDP. |

## Mathematical method
For a polynomial degree d, the model is fit to `t = year - base_year` using NumPy's polynomial fitting. The app reports in-sample R² and MAE, residuals, and (when enough observations exist) a chronological holdout MAE. The holdout is a simple split, not cross-validation. No 95% prediction interval is reported.
